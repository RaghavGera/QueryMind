For revenue, your system should reason from:

quantity × unit_price

rather than simply returning unit_price.

These should deliberately test whether QueryMind asks for clarification instead of hallucinating an interpretation.

Top by spending?

Highest price?

Potential ambiguity:

Last 30 days?
Most recent N orders?

Most orders?
